# Licences and registrations (Uganda)

**Read this first.** This is a guide written from public sources in November 2026. It is not legal advice, and the authors are not lawyers. **No licence has been obtained or added for you.** A licence is issued by a regulator to a real company, so nobody can add one in code. What the app does: it shows this list (Licences page), and it stores each group's real licence number and warns before expiry.

Fees change. Confirm each one with the regulator before paying.

## The big idea

| If you... | Then you are probably... | Licence weight |
|---|---|---|
| Only keep records. Groups pay into **their own** MoMo, Airtel or bank account | A software provider | Light: data protection registration, plus a lawyer's letter |
| Collect money into **your** account, or route it, or run a payment gateway | A payment operator | Heavy: Bank of Uganda licence, capital from UGX 100m |

The app is built for row 1. **Do not add one shared collection account** without Bank of Uganda advice.

No written rule was found that says a records-only app is exempt from payment licences. So get a lawyer's letter. Do not assume.

## A. Your company (do these before real groups join)

| # | What | Who | Why | How to check it is done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register company, get TIN | URSB, URA | You need a legal owner for contracts and registrations | Certificate and TIN in hand |
| 2 | **Register with PDPO**, renew every year | Personal Data Protection Office, Data Protection and Privacy Act (Cap. 97) | You store names, phones and money records. Every data collector, processor or controller must register unless exempted | Certificate from the online portal |
| 3 | Name a Data Protection Officer | Same | Regulator expects a contact person | Name is on your PDPO record |
| 4 | Written record for data abroad | Same | Supabase has no Uganda data centre. Transfer is allowed with consent or an equal-protection country. The app asks for consent when each person joins | A dated file: where data sits and why |
| 5 | **Lawyer's letter: "Are we a payment service?"** | Ugandan lawyer, then Bank of Uganda if unsure | National Payment Systems Act 2020 covers gateways, aggregators and integrators. The letter is your proof | Signed letter |
| 6 | App store forms | Google Play, Apple | Finance apps may be asked who runs them. Keep items 1 to 5 ready | App approved |

Fines under the data law are reported to reach 2% of yearly turnover, and individuals have faced criminal cases.

## B. Each group using the app (their job, not yours)

| Type of group | What it needs | Regulator |
|---|---|---|
| **Savings group / VSLA** | Register as a self-help group at the district | District Community Development Officer, who reports to UMRA |
| **SACCO** | 1) Register as a cooperative. 2) UMRA licence, valid to 31 December, renew at least 90 days before expiry | Registrar of Cooperatives, then UMRA |
| **Large SACCO** | Reported: above UGX 1.5bn voluntary savings **and** UGX 500m institutional capital, Bank of Uganda supervises. Deadline reported moved to 30 September 2026. **The SACCO union disputes this in court**, so get advice | Bank of Uganda |
| **Money lender** | Money lender licence, only if lending to the public | UMRA |

One guide reports UMRA costs as UGX 50,000 to apply and UGX 2,000,000 for the licence. That guide is not official and is unclear, so confirm with UMRA (toll-free 0800 111 449 as listed there).

UMRA also expects SACCOs to send quarterly risk reports. The app's trial balance and arrears figures help, but it does not file for them.

## C. Only if you later move money

| What | Regulator | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Payment system operator or payment service provider licence | Bank of Uganda, National Payment Systems Act 2020 and Regulations 2021 | Reported lowest capital: UGX 100m (funds transfer operator), UGX 250m (e-money issuer). Law says 60 days, often 6 months or more |
| NITA-U certification | NITA-U | Needed before the Bank of Uganda application. Reported fee UGX 72,450 |
| Accountable person registration | Financial Intelligence Authority, Anti-Money Laundering Act | For those who transfer money or take deposits. Keep records 10 years, report suspicious transactions |
| SMS sender ID | Through an SMS provider, with MTN and Airtel | Reported about UGX 250,000 a month per ID. Needs consent and an opt-out list |
